About Us

A Space Where Ideas Come to Life

TEONOX Studio is a professional content creation and recording studio in Kothrud, Pune, built for creators, entrepreneurs, educators, and brands ready to bring their ideas to the screen.

From podcasts and reels to YouTube videos, personal branding, webinars, and online courses, we provide the space, equipment, and production support to help you create with confidence.

Built on Experience. Designed for Creators.

TEONOX Studio is backed by A2 Digital, bringing over 12 years of digital marketing and industry experience into the world of content creation.

Founded by Amit Manglani, TEONOX is built around a simple belief: great ideas deserve the right environment to grow. The studio brings together recording, videography, and editing facilities to make content production more convenient and accessible.

Your Ideas. Our Studio. One Shared Vision.

Whether you're recording your first podcast, building your personal brand, teaching online, or creating content for your business, TEONOX Studio is here to help you turn your vision into content worth sharing.
